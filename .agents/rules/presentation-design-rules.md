# Presentation Slide Design & Cognitive Psychology Rules

These principles govern slide design, typographic hierarchy, spatial proximity, and psychological effectiveness for capstone defense presentations, academic pitches, and stakeholder decks.

---

## 1. Cognitive Psychology Foundations (Why It Matters)

### A. Cognitive Load Theory (Sweller)
- **Extraneous Load Elimination**: The brain has finite working memory. Poor proximity, uneven gaps, cramped layouts, and visual clutter create *extraneous load*, exhausting the panelist's mental energy on visual decoding rather than understanding the system.
- **Germane Processing Support**: Structured chunking (e.g., Rule of 3, bold lead-ins) channels cognitive effort into understanding system value and methodology.

### B. Mayer’s Multimedia Learning Principles
- **Signaling / Cuing Principle**: Viewers learn better when essential cues are highlighted. Use semantic color accents (e.g., warm brand gold/yellow `#EAB308` or violet `#8B5CF6`) strictly on **lead-in keywords/tags**, never on full descriptive paragraphs.
- **Spatial Contiguity Principle**: Keep related labels and descriptions together, but maintain unambiguous negative space between disparate concepts.
- **Redundancy & Dual Coding Principle**: Slides are **visual anchors**, not teleprompters. Text must be scannable within **3 seconds**, allowing the audience to focus their auditory channel on the speaker's defense delivery.

### C. Gestalt Laws of Visual Grouping
- **Law of Proximity**: Spacing defines relationships. 
  - The gap between *Title* and *First Card* must be distinctly larger than line-spacing within the title.
  - The gap between *distinct cards* (`~50px–60px`) must be significantly larger than the internal leading between a card's lead-in and its body text.
- **Law of Similarity**: Shared styling (e.g. all 3 card lead-ins styled with yellow bold text) creates instant cognitive grouping and scannability.
- **Figure-Ground & Negative Space**: Negative space is an active design element. Never crowd slides to the edges; maintain at least `100px` horizontal side margins on 1080p canvases.

## 2. Layout, Coordinates & Typographic Invariants (1080p Canvas: 1920x1080)

1. **Title & Banner Geometry**:
   - Running header / Category tag: `top: ~180px–200px`.
   - Main Slide Headline: `top: ~280px–300px`, `width: >= 1600px` (prevent accidental 2-line wrapping that crashes into body cards).
   - Headline font size: Clean, authoritative clamp (`36px` to `48px`).

2. **Vertical Rhythm & Card Spacing**:
   - Card 1: `top: ~410px` (provides `~50px` clearance from a single-line headline).
   - Card 2: `top: ~570px` (provides `~50px–60px` uniform vertical gutter).
   - Card 3: `top: ~730px` (provides `~50px–60px` uniform vertical gutter).
   - Bottom clearance: Leave at least `150px` of breathing room above the canvas floor (`1080px`).

3. **Color & Hierarchy Invariant**:
   - **Slide Headline**: High-contrast pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or off-white (`#F8FAFC`).
   - **Lead-In Action Badges / Keywords**: Brand accent gold/yellow (`#EAB308` or `#F59E0B`), bold weight.
   - **Body Copy / Descriptions**: Crisp readable white (`#FFFFFF` or `#E2E8F0`), regular weight, never accent colored.
   - **Never Monochromatic Accent**: Never color entire text boxes in accent colors.

4. **Information Architecture (The Rule of 3)**:
   - Cap each slide at **3 core ideas or cards**. The human brain struggles to hold more than 3 to 4 distinct items in working memory simultaneously during rapid spoken delivery.
