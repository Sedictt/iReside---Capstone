# 🎯 iReside Missions: Master Screenshot & Annotation Guide

This guide details all 6 Landlord Missions, the exact screenshots to capture, where to draw arrows/highlights, and where to save each image file.

---

## 📸 Image & Annotation Specifications

- **Resolution**: `1440 × 900 px` or `1920 × 1080 px` (100% browser zoom).
- **File Format**: `.png` (recommended for crisp text and sharp lines).
- **Arrow / Highlight Color**: Coral/Red (`#EF4444`) or Vibrant Cyan (`#06B6D4`) with `3px–4px` stroke.
- **Numbered Badges**: `①`, `②`, `③` with bold white text on high-contrast circle backgrounds.

---

## 📂 Directory Layout

```text
public/
└── missions/
    ├── dashboard/   → step-1.png ... step-7.png (7 steps)
    ├── properties/  → step-1.png ... step-5.png (5 steps)

    ├── finance/     → step-1.png ... step-5.png (5 steps)
    └── operations/  → step-1.png ... step-4.png (4 steps)
```
