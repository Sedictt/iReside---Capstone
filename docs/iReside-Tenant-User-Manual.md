## 1. Introduction & Overview

### 1.1 Welcome to iReside

Welcome to **iReside**, your all-in-one digital tenant portal designed to make your residential living experience seamless, transparent, and convenient. With iReside, you have 24/7 access to everything related to your tenancy:

- **Sign Leases Digitally**: No physical paperwork required.
- **Pay Rent & Utilities**: View invoices and keep track of official receipts.
- **Request Maintenance**: Submit repair tickets with photos and get real-time status updates.
- **Stay Connected**: Message your property manager directly and stay informed about building announcements.
- **Community Living**: Interact with neighbors and review building rules and amenities.

### 1.2 Supported Devices & System Requirements

iReside is a responsive modern web application accessible on:
- **Desktop & Laptops**: Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari, Opera (Latest 2 versions).
- **Smartphones & Tablets**: iOS (Safari / Chrome) and Android (Chrome / Firefox).

### 1.3 Installing iReside as an App (PWA)

You can install iReside directly onto your smartphone home screen or desktop without downloading from an app store:

- **On Android (Google Chrome)**:
  1. Open the iReside URL in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu icon in the top-right corner.
  3. Select **"Install App"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
  4. Follow the prompt to install.
- **On iOS / iPhone (Apple Safari)**:
  1. Open the iReside URL in Safari.
  2. Tap the **Share** button (box with an upward arrow) at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
  4. Tap **Add** in the top right.
- **On Desktop (Chrome/Edge)**:
  1. Click the install icon (computer monitor with download arrow) in the browser address bar.
  2. Click **Install**.

## 2. Account Setup & Getting Started

### 2.1 Receiving Your Invitation & Sign-Up

iReside accounts for tenants are typically provisioned via email invitation from your property manager or created when you submit a formal rental application.

1. Check your email for an invitation from **iReside**.
2. Click the secure verification link inside the email.
3. If prompted, verify your personal details (full name, mobile phone number).

### 2.2 Logging In & First-Time Setup

1. Navigate to the login page: `https://your-ireside-domain.com/login`.
2. Enter your registered **Email Address** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign In**.
4. Upon your first login:
   - You may be prompted to change your temporary password to a secure personal password.
   - You can review and complete your profile information.

### 2.3 Password Recovery

If you ever forget your password:
1. On the login screen, click **Forgot Password?**.
2. Enter your registered email address and click **Send Reset Link**.
3. Check your email inbox (and spam folder) for the password reset email.
4. Click the link and enter a new password (minimum 8 characters, including letters and numbers).
5. Return to the login screen and sign in with your new password.

### 2.4 Navigating the Tenant Workspace & Dark Mode

- **Sidebar Navigation**: The left sidebar contains links to your **Dashboard**, **Lease**, **Payments**, **Maintenance**, **Messages**, **Community**, **Unit Map**, **Utilities**, and **Settings**.
- **Mobile Menu**: On smartphones, tap the hamburger menu icon (☰) in the upper corner to expand the navigation drawer.
- **Dark Mode Toggle**: Click the **Sun / Moon** icon in the sidebar or header to seamlessly switch between Light and Dark themes.

## 3. Rental Applications & Property Tours

### 3.1 Exploring Available Units on the Interactive Map

If you are looking for a unit to rent or browsing other units in the property:
1. Navigate to **Unit Map** from the navigation bar.
2. Select the property building and floor level.
3. Units are color-coded:
   - 🟢 **Green (Vacant)**: Ready for occupancy and open for application.
   - 🔵 **Blue (Occupied)**: Currently occupied.
   - 🔴 **Red (Maintenance)**: Under renovation or maintenance.
4. Click any **Green Unit** to view unit dimensions, bedroom/bathroom count, monthly rental rate, security deposit requirements, and included amenities.

### 3.2 Scheduling a Property Tour

1. Click **Schedule a Tour** on the property or unit view page.
2. Choose your preferred tour type:
   - **In-Person Tour**: Visit the property in person.
   - **Virtual Tour**: Video walkthrough guided by the property manager.
3. Select an available date and time slot from the calendar.
4. Provide any special notes or questions, then click **Book Tour**.
5. You will receive an email confirmation with calendar details.

### 3.3 Submitting a Rental Application

1. Click **Apply Now** on your desired unit or visit `/apply`.
2. Complete the multi-step application form:
   - **Personal Information**: Full legal name, date of birth, current address, contact info.
   - **Employment & Income**: Current employer, monthly salary, proof of income (pay slips or tax returns).
   - **Identification**: Upload clear photos of valid government-issued ID (e.g., Passport, Driver's License, UMID, National ID).
   - **References & Emergency Contact**: Names and contact numbers of previous landlords or character references.
3. Review all details, check the declaration box, and click **Submit Application**.

### 3.4 Tracking Your Application Status

Go to **Applications** (`/tenant/applications`) to monitor progress in real time:
- 🟡 **Pending Review**: Your application has been received and is under review by the landlord.
- 🔵 **Under Screening**: Document and background verification in progress.
- 🟢 **Approved**: Application approved! A lease contract will be generated for your digital signature.
- 🔴 **Rejected**: Application was declined. You can view the landlord’s feedback or contact management.

## 5. Lease Management & Digital Signing

### 5.1 Understanding Your Lease Agreement

Click **Lease** (`/tenant/lease`) in the sidebar to review your current lease terms:
- **Property & Unit Identifier**: Building name, floor, and unit number.
- **Term Duration**: Start date, expiration date, and lease duration.
- **Financial Terms**: Monthly rent amount, due date (e.g., 5th of each month), late payment fees, and security deposit held.
- **Utility Responsibilities**: Specific clauses detailing electricity, water, and internet responsibilities.
- **House Rules & Policies**: Subletting restrictions, pet policies, noise curfews, and visitor rules.

### 5.2 Signing Your Lease Digitally

When a new lease agreement or renewal contract is prepared:
1. You will receive an email notification and dashboard banner: *"Lease Ready for Signature"*.
2. Click **Sign Lease** or navigate to `/tenant/sign-lease`.
3. Read the complete contract thoroughly.
4. Scroll to the signature section at the bottom:
   - On Desktop: Draw your signature using your mouse cursor or trackpad.
   - On Mobile/Tablet: Draw your signature smoothly with your finger or a stylus pen.
   - Alternatively, use the **Type Signature** option if enabled.
5. Check the box agreeing to the digital signature legal terms.
6. Click **Confirm & Sign Lease**.
7. Once your landlord countersigns, the lease status transitions to **Active**.

### 5.3 Accessing & Downloading Signed Lease Documents (PDF)

1. Go to **Lease** (`/tenant/lease`).
2. Click **Download Signed Lease (PDF)**.
3. A legally binding PDF copy containing both tenant and landlord digital signatures, timestamps, and terms will be downloaded to your device.

### 5.4 Lease Renewal & Expiration Process

- **30-60 Days Before Expiration**: You will receive an automated renewal reminder.
- If you wish to renew: Inform your landlord via Messages or respond to the renewal prompt.
- If you plan to vacate: Review Section 13 for Move-Out guidelines.

## 6. Payments & Invoicing

### 6.1 Viewing Your Ledger & Outstanding Invoices

Go to **Payments** (`/tenant/payments`) to access your financial center:
- **Current Balance**: Total amount due across all active invoices.
- **Invoice List**: Itemized breakdown showing Invoice ID, billing period, charge types (Rent, Electric, Water, Parking), due date, and status:
  - 🟢 **Paid**: Settled in full.
  - 🟡 **Pending / Due**: Awaiting payment.
  - 🔴 **Overdue**: Payment has passed the due date; late fees may apply.

### 6.2 Paying Rent & Utilities Online

1. On the **Payments** screen, click **Pay Now** on any unpaid invoice.
2. Select your preferred payment gateway / method:
   - **GCash / Maya / E-Wallets**
   - **Online Bank Transfer / QR Ph**
   - **Credit / Debit Card**
3. Review the transaction summary and fee breakdown (if applicable).
4. Complete the authentication on the secure payment gateway.
5. Upon successful payment:
   - Your invoice status updates immediately to **Paid**.
   - Your dashboard balance is updated to zero.
   - An Official Receipt is generated automatically.

### 6.3 Manual & Offline Payments (Cash / Bank Transfer)

If your building accepts direct over-the-counter payments or bank deposits:
1. Pay your landlord or building admin directly.
2. Provide your proof of payment (deposit slip, bank transfer screenshot, or reference number) via **Messages**.
3. Once verified, your landlord will mark the invoice as **Paid** in the system, and your ledger will update.

### 6.4 Downloading Official Receipts (OR)

1. Navigate to **Payments** -> **Payment History**.
2. Locate the settled transaction.
3. Click the **Download Receipt (PDF)** button.
4. Save or print the PDF receipt for your personal expense records or corporate tax reimbursement.

## 7. Maintenance & Repairs

### 7.1 When to Submit a Maintenance Request

Submit a maintenance request through iReside for any issues with property fixtures, plumbing, electrical systems, building appliances, or structural elements.

### 7.2 Step-by-Step: Creating a Maintenance Ticket

1. Navigate to **Maintenance** (`/tenant/maintenance`) in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ New Maintenance Request**.
3. Complete the form:
   - **Category**: Select the issue type:
     - 🚰 *Plumbing* (e.g., leaking pipe, clogged drain, low water pressure)
     - ⚡ *Electrical* (e.g., outlet short circuit, breaker trip, fixture failure)
     - ❄️ *Appliance / HVAC* (e.g., AC not cooling, refrigerator issue)
     - 🚪 *Structural & Hardware* (e.g., broken window, door lock malfunction)
     - 🐜 *Pest Control*
     - 🛠️ *General Maintenance*
   - **Title**: A clear summary (e.g., *"Bathroom shower mixer leaking"*).
   - **Description**: Detailed explanation of when the issue started, severity, and any symptoms.
   - **Urgency**: Select *Low*, *Medium*, *High*, or *Emergency*.
   - **Upload Photos / Videos**: Attach up to 5 clear photos showing the damaged part to help technicians bring the right tools.
   - **Self-Repair Checkbox**: Check this if you intend to perform the repair yourself and just require landlord approval/materials.
4. Click **Submit Request**.

### 7.3 Emergency vs. Standard Issues

| Urgency Level | Examples | Expected Response Time |
|---|---|---|
| 🚨 **Emergency** | Gas leaks, major pipe burst flooding unit, electrical fire hazard, complete power failure | Within 1–4 Hours (Contact building security immediately) |
| ⚠️ **High** | Refrigerator breakdown, AC failure in hot climate, toilet not flushing | Within 24 Hours |
| 📋 **Standard / Medium** | Slow drain, dripping faucet, cabinet hinge loose | 2–3 Business Days |
| ℹ️ **Low** | Cosmetic paint touch-up, minor squeaks | Next Scheduled Maintenance Cycle |

> [!WARNING]
> In case of active fire, gas leaks, or severe flooding, always prioritize physical safety, evacuate if necessary, and notify building security/hotline directly before submitting a digital ticket.

### 7.4 Opting for Self-Repair

If you are handy and prefer to fix minor items yourself:
1. When submitting the ticket, check the **Self-Repair** option.
2. Specify what parts are needed or if you are requesting reimbursement for materials.
3. The landlord can approve the purchase and apply credit to your next rental invoice upon receipt submission.

### 7.5 Tracking Status & Communicating on Tickets

- **Pending Review**: Landlord has received the ticket.
- **In Progress / Assigned**: A technician or contractor has been scheduled.
- **Resolved**: Work is completed by the technician.
- **Closed**: You confirm the repair is satisfactory.

Click into any ticket to post comments, add follow-up photos, or coordinate technician access times.

### 7.6 Closing & Rating Maintenance Service

Once the technician completes the work:
1. Open the maintenance ticket.
2. Review the landlord's resolution notes.
3. Click **Confirm Resolution**.
4. Rate the service (1 to 5 stars) and leave feedback.

## 8. Utilities Management

### 8.1 Viewing Meter Readings (Electricity, Water, Gas)

Go to **Utilities** (`/tenant/utilities`) in the sidebar:
- View latest recorded meter readings for your specific unit.
- See previous meter reading vs. current meter reading.
- View photos of the physical meter uploaded during meter inspection.

### 8.2 Understanding Utility Breakdown & Consumption Trends

The Utilities page provides graphical consumption tracking:
- **Monthly Usage Graph**: Compare your kilowatt-hours (kWh) and cubic meters ($m^3$) month-over-month.
- **Rate Calculation**: Displays the exact rate per unit charged (e.g., ₱12.50 per kWh, ₱45.00 per $m^3$).
- **Submeter Formula**:
  $$\text{Utility Due} = (\text{Current Reading} - \text{Previous Reading}) \times \text{Unit Rate}$$

### 8.3 Utility Invoicing Cycles

Utility charges are automatically attached to your monthly rental invoice or billed as separate submeter invoices depending on your property's billing cycle.

## 13. Move-Out & Security Deposit Settlement

### 13.1 Initiating a Move-Out Request

When your lease is concluding or you need to vacate:
1. Navigate to **Lease** or contact your landlord at least **30 days** in advance (as specified in your lease agreement).
2. Submit your **Notice of Intent to Vacate**.
3. Schedule your preferred **Move-Out Inspection Date**.

### 13.2 Move-Out Inspection Checklist

Before your inspection date, ensure:
- [ ] All personal belongings and furniture are removed.
- [ ] Unit is cleaned (floors swept, bathroom disinfected, kitchen degreased).
- [ ] All original keys, keycards, and parking transponders are ready for handover.
- [ ] Any tenant-caused wall holes or damages are repaired.
- [ ] Final utility meter readings are documented.

### 13.3 Deposit Deductions & Final Refund Settlement

1. The landlord performs the walkthrough inspection and uploads the report to iReside.
2. Any deductions (e.g., unpaid final utilities, required deep cleaning, or damage repairs) will be itemized in your Move-Out Settlement summary.
3. Review the settlement statement.
4. Once agreed, your remaining security deposit will be refunded via bank transfer or cheque according to lease terms.

## 15. Troubleshooting & FAQs

### 15.1 Login & Authentication Issues

- **Problem**: *"Invalid credentials"* error.
  - *Fix*: Check for typos in your email; ensure Caps Lock is off. Use the **Forgot Password** feature if necessary.
- **Problem**: Verification email not received.
  - *Fix*: Check your Spam/Junk folder. Whitelist notifications from the iReside domain.

### 15.2 Payment & Receipt Problems

- **Problem**: Paid online but invoice still shows "Due".
  - *Fix*: Allow 2–5 minutes for webhook processing. Refresh the browser page. If it persists, send your payment transaction ID to your landlord via Messages.
- **Problem**: Cannot download receipt PDF.
  - *Fix*: Disable popup blockers in your browser settings and try clicking Download again.

### 15.3 Digital Signature Troubleshooting

- **Problem**: Signature canvas is unresponsive on mobile.
  - *Fix*: Ensure you are touching within the designated boxed signature area. Refresh the page or try rotating your phone to landscape mode.

### 15.4 Offline Mode Behavior

iReside utilizes Progressive Web App (PWA) caching:
- If your internet connection drops, you can still view previously loaded lease details and building contacts.
- Actions requiring server sync (submitting new maintenance requests, payments) will prompt you to reconnect once online.

### 15.5 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Can I add my roommate or co-tenant to my account?**  
A: Contact your landlord to have an additional tenant profile linked to your unit so they can receive notices.

**Q: Where do I find my building's Wi-Fi or access code?**  
A: Check the **Community** notice board or ask the **iRis Assistant**.

**Q: How do I change my rent payment method?**  
A: You can choose your payment method each time you click **Pay Now** on an active invoice.
