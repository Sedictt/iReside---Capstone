Chapter 1: About iReside
1.1 What is iReside?
iReside is a web and mobile software application designed to make managing and living in rental properties simple, fair, and organized.
In traditional apartments and boarding houses in the Philippines, landlords and tenants often struggle with:
- Lost paper receipts and disputed payments.
- Confusing utility calculations when water and electricity submeters are read by hand.
- Unclear verbal repair requests that get forgotten.
- Paper contracts that can be misplaced or damaged.
iReside solves these problems by providing a single, shared digital record. Both the property owner and the resident can see rent dues, water and electricity meter readings, payment receipts, and repair status in real time.
1.2 Who Uses iReside? (User Roles)
To keep the system straightforward and free of unnecessary administrative layers, iReside has only two user types:

1. The Landlord (Property Owner / Manager):
The person who owns or manages the rental building. They set up the rooms, enter monthly submeter readings, review tenant GCash payments, and dispatch repair technicians.
2. The Tenant (Resident):
The person renting a room or apartment unit. They use their phone or computer to check their monthly bill, pay using GCash, upload payment screenshots, submit repair requests with photos, and talk with the landlord.

Chapter 2: Getting Started & Account Access
2.1 How to Open iReside (Computers, Phones, and Tablets)
iReside works across everyday devices without requiring expensive hardware:
1. On a Desktop Computer or Laptop:
Open Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox, and visit the website address provided by your landlord (for example: https://ireside.ph or http://localhost:3000).
2. On an Android Smartphone:
- You can visit the website using Google Chrome.
- Or install the dedicated Android App (.apk) from the download page (/download).
3. On an Apple iPhone or iPad:
- Open Safari and visit the website.
- Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up), scroll down, and tap "Add to Home Screen". This places an iReside icon on your phone just like a regular app!
2.2 Creating an Account & Signing In
For Landlords:
1. Open iReside and click Get Started or Sign In.
2. Enter your email address and create a secure password (at least 6 characters).
3. Tap Sign Up. You will be directed to the initial setup screen to create your first property.
For Tenants:
1. Tenants do not need to register from scratch. Your landlord will send you a Personal Invitation Link via email or SMS.
2. Tap the invitation link.
3. Type your chosen password, confirm your contact details, and tap Complete Setup. You will immediately see your rented room and your current account status.
2.3 Your Emergency Spare Key (Never Get Locked Out)


How it works:
- When you first register, the screen presents your Emergency Spare Key.
- Click "Download Key & Continue" (which automatically saves a copy to your computer or phone) or write the letters down on a piece of paper.
- If you ever forget your password or lose access to your email, click "Use Emergency Spare Key" on the sign-in screen, enter your code, and you will regain access to your account right away.
2.4 Completing Your Profile (Name, Phone, and Photo)
Having complete profile details ensures both landlords and tenants can easily reach one another:
1. Tap your Avatar / Circle Icon in the top navigation bar and select My Profile.
2. Make sure your Full Name and Mobile Phone Number (Philippine mobile format: 09XXXXXXXXX) are typed correctly.
3. You can choose a friendly profile photo or use the built-in color avatar.
4. Tap Save Profile.

Chapter 3: Landlord Initial Setup (The 5-Step Process)
When a landlord signs up for the first time, iReside guides them through a simple, numbered 5-step onboarding path. You cannot get lost because each step is clearly marked.

3.1 Step 1 of 5: Property Setup (Adding Your Building & Rooms)
Where: /landlord/properties/new
1. Building Name: Type the name of your rental building (for example: Villa Marulas Apartments or Santos Boarding House).
2. Address: Enter the street, Barangay, and city (for example: 123 MacArthur Highway, Barangay Marulas, Valenzuela City).
3. Number of Floors: Select how many floors your building has (e.g., 2 or 3 floors).
4. Add Rooms / Units:
- Click Add Unit.
- Enter the Unit Number (e.g., Room 101, Unit 2A).
- Enter the Floor Level (e.g., Floor 1).
- Enter the Monthly Base Rent (e.g., ₱8,500).
- Enter how many persons can live there (Capacity: e.g., 2 persons).
5. Click Save Property.
3.2 Step 2 of 5: Interactive 2D Unit Map (Arranging Your Floorplan)
Where: /landlord/unit-map
The Interactive Unit Map gives you a visual view of your building floors, just like looking at a building plan from above.

1. Viewing Units: Each room is shown as an easy-to-read card.
2. Color Meanings:
- 🟢 Green (Vacant): The room is empty and ready for a new tenant.
- 🔴 Red (Occupied): A tenant is currently living here.
- 🟡 Yellow (Reserved / Pending): A tenant is in the process of signing their lease.
- 🔵 Blue (Maintenance): The room is being cleaned or repaired.
3. Arranging Rooms: You can select a layout preset (such as "Single Row" or "Double Row") or drag and drop room cards to match your physical hallway.
4. Tapping a Room: Click any room to see who lives there, their contact number, and if their rent is paid.
5. Click Proceed to Next Step.
3.3 Step 3 of 5: Water & Electricity Billing (Setting Utility Rates)
Where: /landlord/utility-billing
In the Philippines, landlords commonly read submeters for electricity (kilowatt-hours / kWh) and water (cubic meters / m³). iReside calculates these bills automatically so you never have to use a calculator.
1. Electricity Tariff Rate: Type the cost per kilowatt-hour (e.g., ₱13.50 per kWh).
2. Water Tariff Rate: Type the cost per cubic meter (e.g., ₱45.00 per m³).
3. Monthly Billing Day: Choose the day of the month when bills are due (for example: Every 5th of the month).
4. Tap Save Billing Rates.
3.4 Step 4 of 5: Tenant Onboarding (Inviting Your First Resident)
Where: /landlord/tenants
You have three easy options to add a tenant:
- Option A: Invite Link (Recommended):
Select the room, type the tenant's email or phone number, and click Create Invite Link. You can copy this link and send it to them via SMS, Messenger, or WhatsApp.
- Option B: Quick Add:
Type the tenant's full name, contact number, assigned room, and monthly rent directly.
- Option C: Walk-in Application:
For elderly or walk-in tenants who do not use email, you can register their details in person on your screen.
Setting Move-in Terms:
You can specify:
- Advance Rent: (e.g., 1 Month)
- Security Deposit: (e.g., 1 or 2 Months)
- Any utility deposit or move-in fees.
3.5 Step 5 of 5: Today's Overview & Guided Tour
Where: /landlord/dashboard
Once setup is complete, you will arrive at Today's Overview (your daily command center).
- An interactive Guided Tour with a friendly purple ring will highlight the main buttons on your screen.
- You can follow along or tap "Finish Tour" to begin managing your property.
Chapter 4: Daily & Monthly Landlord Operations
4.1 Today's Overview (Monitoring Occupancy and Rent)
When you log in every morning, Today's Overview shows you everything that needs your attention in plain language:

- All Good! No Issues Today: Shows when all rents are paid and there are no broken facilities.
- Needs Attention: Alerts you if there are pending GCash payments waiting for your approval, overdue tenants, or new repair requests.
- Quick Actions & Tools: Handy shortcut buttons to:
- Record Submeter Readings
- Verify GCash Payments
- Add New Tenant
- Create Announcement
4.2 Recording Water & Electricity Submeter Readings
Where: Click Water & Electricity from the sidebar menu.
At the end of every billing month:
1. Walk to your building's submeters with your phone or tablet.
2. In the Water & Electricity screen, find each room.
3. Type the Current Electricity Reading (e.g., 1450).
4. Type the Current Water Reading (e.g., 210).
5. The screen automatically calculates:
- Consumption = Current Reading − Last Month's Reading
- Total Cost = Consumption × Your Tariff Rate
6. Automatic Safety Warning: If you accidentally type an impossible number (for example, a reading that is lower than last month, or 3 times higher than usual), the system highlights the box in orange and asks you to double-check.
7. Click Post & Bill Invoices. The bills are immediately sent to each tenant's account!
4.3 Rent & Payments (Approving GCash Transfers & Issuing Receipts)
Where: Click Rent & Payments from the sidebar menu.
When a tenant pays their rent using GCash, it appears in your Needs Approval list:

1. Click Review Payment next to the tenant's name.
2. Look at the tenant's uploaded GCash screenshot on the left side of your screen.
3. Check your own phone's GCash text message (SMS) or GCash app to make sure the money arrived.
4. If the money arrived: Click the green Approve & Issue Receipt button.
- The tenant's balance is automatically cleared to ₱0.
- An Official Receipt is generated instantly with the date, time, and reference number.
5. If the screenshot is wrong or money did not arrive: Click Reject and type a short explanation (e.g., "Reference number not found in my GCash SMS. Please check and re-submit").
4.4 Managing Lease Contracts & Digital Signatures
Where: Click Lease Contracts from the sidebar menu.
1. Creating a Lease: Click Create Lease, pick the tenant and room, and set the start and end dates (e.g., 1 Year: June 1, 2026 to May 31, 2027).
2. Reviewing Terms: The standard contract includes house rules, payment due dates, and deposit return terms.
3. Tenant Signs First: The tenant reviews the document on their phone and draws their signature using their finger or a stylus.
4. Landlord Countersigns: Once the tenant signs, click Countersign Lease, draw your signature, and click Activate Lease.
5. Both you and the tenant can download and print a clean copy of the signed contract at any time.
4.5 Repairs & Maintenance (Handling Tenant Help Tickets)
Where: Click Repairs & Maintenance from the sidebar menu.
When a tenant reports a problem (like a leaking pipe or burned-out hallway light):
1. A red badge appears on Repairs & Maintenance.
2. Tap the ticket to open it:
- View the room number, issue category (Plumbing, Electrical, etc.), and urgency.
- Look at the Photos attached by the tenant to see exactly what is broken.
3. Change the status to In Progress when you call your technician or plumber.
4. When fixed, tap Mark as Completed. The tenant receives an immediate notification.
4.6 Tenant Directory & Community Announcements
- Tenant Directory: A complete, searchable phonebook of every resident living in your building, their emergency contacts, and their move-in dates.
- Community Announcements: Need to announce a water service interruption or scheduled pest control? Tap New Announcement, type your message (e.g., "Maynilad water interruption this Thursday from 1 PM to 5 PM"), and click Broadcast. Every tenant sees it at the top of their dashboard!
4.7 Move-Out Settlement & Security Deposit Refunds
When a resident decides to move out:
1. Navigate to Leases → select the tenant → click Move-Out Settlement.
2. Review the Security Deposit held on file (e.g., ₱16,000).
3. If there are unpaid utility bills or physical damages to the room, enter the deduction amounts and attach photos of the damage.
4. The system calculates:
Final Refund = Deposit - (Unpaid Bills + Damage Repairs)
5. Enter the GCash or bank reference number used to return the remaining money.
6. A digital Move-Out Settlement Voucher is saved for your tax and accounting records.
Chapter 5: Tenant User Guide (Resident Experience)
5.1 Joining Your Rental Home & Signing Your Digital Lease
When moving in:
1. Open the Invitation Link sent by your landlord on your phone.
2. Review your assigned room, monthly rent, and move-in terms.
3. Type your contact information and create your password.
4. Signing Your Lease: Scroll through the lease terms on your screen. At the bottom, use your finger to draw your signature inside the signing box. Tap Submit Signed Agreement.
5. Once your landlord signs, your home portal is active!
5.2 Resident Dashboard (Understanding Your Home Screen)
When you open iReside, your dashboard gives you an instant summary of your home:

- Current Total Amount Due: Shows if you have an outstanding bill or if your account is fully paid (₱0.00 Balance).
- Pay with GCash Button: Quick 1-tap payment access.
- View Bill Breakdown: See exactly how much is rent, how much is electricity, and how much is water.
5.3 Viewing Your Bills & Understanding the Cost Breakdown
Tap Bills & Receipts to see your itemized monthly billing:

You always know where every single peso goes.
5.4 Paying Rent Using GCash (Step-by-Step with Receipt Upload)
Paying rent takes less than 2 minutes using your mobile phone:
1. On your resident dashboard, tap Pay with GCash.
2. Scan or Copy:
- You will see your landlord's official GCash QR Code and mobile number.
- Scan the QR code or copy the number into your GCash app.
3. Send Payment in GCash:
- In GCash, send the exact amount due.
- Important: Take a clear Screenshot of the completed GCash transaction screen.
4. Submit Proof in iReside:
- Return to iReside.
- Type the 13-digit GCash Reference Number (found at the top or bottom of your GCash receipt).
- Tap Attach Screenshot and choose the photo you just took.
- Tap Submit Payment Proof.
5. Your payment status will now show "Pending Landlord Verification". As soon as your landlord confirms the payment, your status changes to "Paid"!
5.5 Downloading Your Official Receipts
- Tap Receipts from your menu.
- Every approved payment has a digital Official Receipt stamped with date, time, and reference code.
- Tap Download Receipt (PDF) or Print if you need a paper copy for your employer or personal files.
5.6 Asking for Repairs (Filing a Ticket with Photos)
Something broken in your room? You do not have to wait until you see the landlord in person:
1. Tap Repairs & Maintenance → tap + New Request.
2. Pick the issue:
- Water / Plumbing (leaking sink, clogged drain, toilet flush)
- Electrical (light switch, power outlet, circuit breaker)
- Door / Window / Locks
- Other
3. Type a short description (e.g., "The bathroom faucet is dripping continuously").
4. Tap Take Photo / Attach Photo and snap a picture of the issue.
5. Tap Send Request.
6. You can check the ticket anytime to see when the repairman is scheduled to visit.
5.7 In-App Chat & Community Notice Board
- Chat with Landlord: Send a direct message to your landlord without having to search for their personal social media accounts.
- Community Notice Board: Read building announcements, trash collection schedules, and neighbor updates in one clean feed.

Chapter 7: Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting
7.1 What should I do if I forget my password?
1. On the login screen, click "Forgot Password?" to receive a reset link in your email.
2. If you do not have access to your email, click "Use Emergency Spare Key".
3. Type the 16-character code you saved when you first created your account (e.g., RESIDE-7K9B-4X2M-99PQ).
4. You will immediately be allowed to enter a new password and log in.
7.2 What if my GCash payment was rejected or reference number was wrong?
1. Open your resident dashboard and tap Bills & Receipts.
2. The rejected payment will show a note from your landlord explaining why (for example: "Reference number does not match screenshot").
3. Tap Re-Submit Payment, double-check the 13-digit number from your GCash app, upload the correct screenshot, and tap Submit.
7.3 What if the electric or water meter reading looks unusual?
1. iReside has an automatic alert: if a meter reading is entered that is unusually high or lower than the previous month, the box turns orange.
2. The landlord can re-inspect the physical submeter dial, correct the numbers, and tap Save.
3. If an incorrect bill was already posted, the landlord can adjust the meter reading directly in the Water & Electricity tab before payment is approved.
7.4 How do I print a paper copy of a receipt or monthly report?
1. In any receipt or report screen, click the Print / Export PDF button.
2. Your browser will open the standard print preview dialog.
3. Select your printer to print on paper, or select "Save as PDF" to save a file on your computer or phone.
