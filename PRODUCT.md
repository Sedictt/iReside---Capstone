# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Landlords, Tenants, and Admins managing property operations, tenant requests, and utility/rent bills. Users need an interface that is extremely clear and scannable but physically satisfying to interact with, supporting high task completion confidence.

## Product Purpose
iReside is an operational property management platform. It streamlines property listings, tenant onboardings, bill payments, and maintenance requests. Success is defined by rapid scan-and-act task flows and a highly engaging, high-end portal feel.

## Brand Personality
Tactile, Comforting, and Trustworthy. Uses a sophisticated Neumorphic visual language with physical-like soft shadows and subtle highlights, paired with clean, sage-green brand accents and premium micro-interactions.

## Anti-references
- No generic, flat warm-cream/beige backgrounds (monoculture SaaS style).
- No hard, multi-pixel side-stripe accent borders on cards or panels.
- No text overflow or layout breakages at tablet/mobile breakpoints.
- No unstyled browser forms or heavy, flat tables.

## Design Principles
- **Tactile Comfort**: Emphasize touch and physical depth via high-end soft shadows, letting interactive elements feel distinct from their canvas.
- **Clarified Density**: Optimize details and data tables for high efficiency, without cluttering the visual hierarchy.
- **Sage and Calm Accentuation**: Keep standard neutrals pristine and brand-tinted, using the sage green accent exclusively for active highlights and key CTA guidance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratio ≥4.5:1 for all readable copy.
- Clear `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` alternatives for every custom physical transit or transition.
- High tap targets (minimum 44x44px for main controls) to complement the tactile look.
